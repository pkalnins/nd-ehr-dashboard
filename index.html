Study Abstract

Background. EHR data from community-based naturopathic clinics are a largely untapped resource for health services research. Prior studies relied on billing data, which do not capture the clinical narrative documentation central to naturopathic care — including supplement recommendations, lifestyle counseling, and integrative treatment rationale.

Purpose. To establish and validate a privacy-first, cloud-based informatics pipeline for multi-clinic naturopathic EHR data, and to demonstrate its analytic utility through glycemic cohort analysis and treatment pattern characterization.

Methods. FHIR APIs were used to ingest structured and unstructured EHR data from seven CharmHealth-based naturopathic clinics in Washington State into Google Cloud Platform (GCP) under a HIPAA BAA. De-identification was performed using the GCP Healthcare De-identification API, supplemented by a custom 9-pass rule-based NLP redaction pipeline verified by manual safety audit. De-identified clinical notes (n=858,394) were converted into a structured treatment table using rule-based keyword NLP (13 categories; PPV 97.9%) and loaded into BigQuery.

Results. The database contains 58,498 patients, 469,162 encounters, and 3.8 million observations across 7 clinics (2012–2026); 67.5% female, mean age 42.6 years. The most common conditions were fatigue, insomnia, anxiety, hyperlipidemia, and constipation. Treatment pattern analysis (n=43,747 note patients) showed supplement/botanical recommendations in 61.6% of patients, followed by lab/diagnostic orders (35.4%), diet/nutrition counseling (30.5%), and referrals (25.8%). A glycemic cohort (HbA1c-defined, not diagnosis codes) included diabetes (≥6.5%, n=355), prediabetes (5.7–6.4%, n=2,254), and normoglycemic (<5.7%, n=10,127) patients. In the longitudinal subset (≥2 readings), the diabetes group showed a mean HbA1c reduction of −0.766% (7.97% → 7.21%; p<0.001; mean follow-up 671 days); the prediabetes group showed −0.131% (5.87% → 5.74%; p<0.001; 684 days), with durable stabilization maintained through 24+ months.

Conclusions. This pilot demonstrates that a scalable, HIPAA-compliant, cloud-native infrastructure can transform naturopathic EHR data — including free-text clinical narrative — into a structured research dataset supporting longitudinal and treatment pattern analyses. The glycemic findings support the feasibility of future comparative effectiveness research using naturopathic EHR data at scale.
